Without the DPI fix the failures are the enlarge operations: maximize failed in 6 / 6 runs across
the series (content 909 of 1369 rows, about 2/3, status bar absent, still so 10 s later), and the
same 2/3 signature appears on resize-larger, drags to a larger size, the resize stream and restore.
With the DPI fix no capture failed in the three on-screen runs.

"not decisive" counts captures where Windows denied the foreground to the test window, or where the
enlarged window extended past the bottom of the display so the screen capture could not see it.
Those are harness conditions, recorded rather than scored: a blit-coverage trace showed the game
painting to the last framebuffer row in exactly those states.
