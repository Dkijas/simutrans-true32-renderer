# Known issues — XR32 testing-7 (r12377)

testing-7 = testing-6 + the special colour fallback fix: a generated climate or snow tile no longer falls back to its
16-bit (RGB555) picture because a pixel of `LightTexture` or `ClimateTexture` happens to equal one of Simutrans' 31
special colours. Nothing else changes; the XR32 format is unchanged. XR32 remains **experimental** and is not
integrated into Simutrans Standard.

**Measurement scope.** Unless an item says otherwise, results were measured on the Windows x64 executables of this
release (SDL2 and SDL3, or their lab twins built from the same source with the measuring hooks) on one machine with one
display. The Linux builds are experimental and not certified (K8).

## A. Of testing-7

### K1. makie's older LightTexture is not available

The PAK128.german light map behind makie's original report (corners drawn at 16 bits, blue dots) is not available. The
cause rests on makie's own diagnosis in the forum (special colours in `LightTexture`, naming `6b6b6b`), on the blue dots
of the capture, and on our lab reproduction, all of which agree. The fix was verified on lab paks made to reproduce the
fault (`6B6B6B`, `9B9B9B`, `4D4D4D` and every other key), not on that file byte for byte. This is converging evidence, not
an exact reproduction.

### K2. PAK128.german as published shows no difference

PAK128.german 2.5 beta as published on 6 November 2026 has no such colours in its terrain sources. testing-7 draws it
exactly as testing-6 does. The visible improvement of its corners is the pak's own new light map.

### K3. No artist review of this build

makie's statements are about makie's own files on testing-6. Nobody outside the lab has tested this build on a pak
with such colours. makie has not reviewed or tested testing-7.

### K4. Coast water keeps the 16-bit picture where the light map has such a colour — out of scope

On slopes whose `LightTexture` row contains such a colour, the land is now 32-bit but the coast water of those slopes
stays at 16 bits. Same cause, different code path (the water tile is a binary tile). A separate fix exists in the lab,
**not certified and not part of this build**. No published pak is known to be affected.

### K5. Extra composition cost where the fix acts

On a pak with such colours, a zoom step costs **2 to 3 ms more** than in testing-6, and the slowest frames of a light
change up to about 3 ms more. Ordinary frames are unchanged; paks without such colours show no measurable difference
(22 of 24 comparisons equivalent, the other two 0.07 and 0.15 ms faster, below anything to read into; n = 5 per case,
idle machine, Windows Search running). This is the 32-bit composition that testing-6 skipped by falling back,
and it equals what a pak without such colours already costs. The proposed cache A12-P1 is not implemented.

### K6. Declared semantics keep the fallback

A company colour or light declared by the artist in a mask on a terrain source, semi-transparent pixels, and nodes
generated by makeobj's night generator keep the previous behaviour (16 bits). Their meaning on a generated tile is not
defined; this is a lab-only case.

### K7. A semi-transparent pixel in LightTexture crashes the game at load

Also in official r12377, without XR32. Not changed in this build.

## B. Platforms

### K8. Platforms

| Platform | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Windows x64 SDL2 and SDL3 | **certified with limitations**: one machine, one 3440×1440 display, one GPU, MinGW builds, no physical keyboard |
| Linux x86_64 SDL2 and SDL3 | **experimental, not certified**. Built from the same source with the release recipe; checked only for architecture, libraries, start and clean exit on WSL 2 with SDL's dummy driver (including a lab pak that takes the path of the fix) and one WSLg X11 window. WSLg is not a native Linux desktop; no picture is certified on Linux |
| macOS, Android, ARM, native Linux desktop, several displays | not tested |

### K9. Keyboard

Zoom by keyboard was checked with keys sent by test tools to the focused game window, on SDL2 and SDL3: exact, also
round trips, the same in testing-6 and testing-7. SDL3 delivers typed characters only to the focused window, so a key
sent to an unfocused window is lost; that is SDL3's behaviour, not a defect of this build. A physical keyboard was not
tested.

### K10. One screen-update mismatch seen once

During the release gate, one SDL3 run of the window-events scenario (coast world, PAK128.german 2.5 beta, which has no
such colours, so the fix does not act) had 8 of its 60 checked frames differ from what the game drew, by at most 29
pixels. The machine was being used by another application at the time. The same scenario was repeated on an idle
machine, first for the gate (720 frames) and again for this publication (1,200 frames, n = 5 per program and backend):
0 mismatches on testing-6 and on testing-7, SDL2 and SDL3, while a renderer that deliberately drops screen updates is
caught in every run. The cause of the single event is not established; it is not attributed to testing-7.

## C. Inherited (unchanged)

- Everything in `docs/KNOWN-ISSUES-r12377-testing-6.md`: the 32-level transition weights, the brighter ground of paks
  with 32-bit terrain, snow clipping on strongly lit slopes, the light-change and zoom-step cost of A12, the zoom colour
  residual of translucent company colours, the memory of night pictures, the experimental DAY/NIGHT syntax, and the
  history of Microsoft Defender and testing-4 (D1).
- **testing-7 and Microsoft Defender:** every asset and every executable inside the packages was scanned with
  definitions 1.463.189.0: no threats.
- **From the official trunk r12377 itself (both renderers, not caused by XR32):** founding a city next to a road with a
  private-way sign can crash the game; scenario text files can come out empty; the chat's public tab shows whispers;
  the music can play very quietly after switching it off and on; Windows 8 or newer is required (r12361).
- **Code base:** official Simutrans SVN trunk r12377; no later trunk revision is included.
